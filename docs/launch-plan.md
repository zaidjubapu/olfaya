# OLFAYA launch plan

## Positioning

**OLFAYA is the first perfume house built in the AI era for India and the Gulf.** Heritage materials (attar, oud, saffron, mogra, frankincense, sandalwood) composed with AI and finished by hand.

- **Promise:** a scent that is truly yours. Every customer can get a one-of-one bespoke blend from their Scent DNA.
- **Proof:** Aya, the AI concierge that speaks English, Hindi and Arabic; the Scent DNA quiz; hand-blending in the atelier.
- **Price tier:** accessible luxury. ₹6,400–9,900 / AED 289–449 for 50ml, sitting above mass brands and below the Western niche houses that dominate both markets.
- **Audience:** 22–40, urban, digitally native, wears fragrance daily and layers. India: Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Hyderabad. GCC: Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Riyadh, Jeddah, Doha, Kuwait City.

## Launch sequence

| Phase | Weeks | Goal | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tease | -4 to -1 | 5,000 waitlist sign-ups | Hero film, "What does your memory smell like?" UGC prompt, waitlist on the site |
| Launch | 0 to 2 | First 1,000 orders | Discovery Set as the entry product, Scent DNA quiz pushed in every ad, creator seeding |
| Bespoke | 3 to 8 | Make bespoke the hero | "My Scent DNA" share cards, gifting push |
| Festive | Diwali, Ramadan, Eid, National Day | Peak revenue | Gift boxes, Arabic-first creatives in GCC, wedding-season content in India |

## Channels

- **Instagram + Reels (both markets)** carry the hero film, product macros, Scent DNA reveals. Budget split 55% India / 45% GCC.
- **Snapchat (KSA, UAE)** is the priority paid channel in Saudi. Use vertical 6-second cuts and AR scent-aura lens.
- **YouTube Shorts (India)** for "Scent DNA reveal" creator videos.
- **WhatsApp Business** for order confirmation, COD verification and Aya as a concierge.
- **Creators:** 20 micro (20–150k) per market seeded with the Discovery Set; 3 macro in month two once reviews exist.
- **Marketplaces:** Nykaa Luxe (India), Namshi and Noon (GCC) after the DTC launch.

## Higgsfield production kit

All prompts share one look so the brand stays consistent. Paste the **brand anchor** at the end of every prompt.

> **Brand anchor:** faceted obsidian-black glass perfume bottle with a brushed champagne-gold cap and a thin iridescent violet-teal-amber light ring, minimal gold OLFAYA wordmark; palette of deep ink black, warm champagne gold and soft iridescence; photoreal luxury fragrance campaign, cinematic lighting, shallow depth of field.

Use the master bottle image already in your Higgsfield library (the one on black basalt) as the image reference for every product shot and image-to-video clip.

### 1. Hero film (15s, 16:9 and 9:16)

Generate as three image-to-video shots and cut together.

1. **Desert night, 5s:** "Slow push-in across a dark desert dune at night toward a single perfume bottle standing on the sand. Golden threads of light rise from the cap and weave into a faint neural network pattern dissolving into the stars. Gentle wind moves the sand. [brand anchor]"
2. **Monsoon to oud, 5s:** "Macro shot: a raindrop falls in slow motion onto red earth beside the bottle; the splash turns into floating saffron threads and rose petals that swirl around the glass and catch golden light. [brand anchor]"
3. **Reveal, 5s:** "The bottle rotates slowly on polished black basalt, its facets catching an iridescent rim light; the light ring on the cap pulses once. Camera pulls back to reveal it centered in darkness. [brand anchor]"

End card text (add in the editor, not in the generation): **OLFAYA · Scent, composed by intelligence.** Arabic version: **أولفايا · عطرٌ يُؤلَّف بالذكاء**.

## Before launch checklist

- Register olfaya.com (free as of 5 Oct 2026) and olfaya.ai; check olfaya.in and olfaya.ae with a registrar.
- File the OLFAYA trademark in India (Class 3) and UAE/KSA.
- Get perfume samples made by a contract perfumer (Kannauj or Dubai) using the notes in `src/lib/catalog.ts` as the brief.
- IFRA compliance and product labelling for each market (BIS in India, ESMA/ECAS in UAE, SFDA in KSA).
- Payment: Razorpay for India; Tap or Checkout.com for GCC; Tabby/Tamara for pay-later.
